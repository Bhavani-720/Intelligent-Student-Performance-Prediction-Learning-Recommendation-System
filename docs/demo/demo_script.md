# Trekverse AI — 60-90 Second Product Demonstration Video Script & Guide

## Tagline: Predict. Understand. Improve. Succeed.

This script is structured for technical portfolio presentations, recruiter demonstrations, hackathon pitches, and engineering interviews.

---

## Technical Specifications
- **Recommended Video Duration:** 75 – 90 seconds
- **Recording Resolution:** 1920 × 1080 (1080p, 60fps recommended)
- **Theme:** Trekverse AI Dark Theme (`#070D18` navy background)
- **Audio:** Clear, measured tone with noise suppression enabled

---

## Timed Scene-by-Scene Walkthrough Script

### Scene 1: Platform Introduction & Executive Overview (0:00 – 0:15)
- **Screen Action:** Open `http://localhost:3000`. Show the Executive Overview Dashboard with live KPI cards and animated sparklines. Toggle the Department filter from "All" to "Computer Science".
- **Spoken Narration:**
  > *"Welcome to Trekverse AI — Student Academic Intelligence & Early-Warning System. Trekverse AI bridges machine learning and institutional student retention. Built on React 18, Express, and Scikit-Learn, our platform tracks 395 verified student records across 19 educational, behavioral, and psychological dimensions with zero student PII."*

---

### Scene 2: Institutional Risk Stratification & Early Warning (0:15 – 0:30)
- **Screen Action:** Click **Risk Classification** in the sidebar. Highlight the KPI badges: 219 Low, 21 Moderate, 87 High, and 68 Critical. Click on a student to show the Faculty Intervention tracker.
- **Spoken Narration:**
  > *"Our deterministic early-warning policy partitions the cohort into four disjoint tiers. Exactly 155 students in High and Critical risk require immediate remediation, while 176 fall into elevated risk. Faculty can dispatch structured interventions, tracking statuses in real time without synthetic grade assumptions."*

---

### Scene 3: Predictive Modeling & What-If Academic Simulator (0:30 – 0:48)
- **Screen Action:** Click **Performance Prediction**. Click the **Borderline** preset. Adjust the Weekly Study Hours slider from 11 to 16 hours. Point to the live score change, 95% Confidence Interval, and SHAP drivers. Select "Mathematics" to demonstrate the subject-specific training disclaimer.
- **Spoken Narration:**
  > *"In the Performance Prediction module, the What-If Academic Simulator lets advisors test hypothetical scenarios. Our Random Forest engine calculates expected final exam marks, 95% confidence intervals, and local SHAP feature impacts in under 12 milliseconds. Notice our automated disclaimer when subject-level training data is required."*

---

### Scene 4: Explainable AI & Unsupervised Clustering (0:48 – 1:05)
- **Screen Action:** Navigate to **Explainable AI** and **Clustering Personas**. Show the SHAP global importance ranking (Midterm 2 at 81.98%, Midterm 1 at 10.13%) and the 3 K-Means behavioral personas.
- **Spoken Narration:**
  > *"Transparency is paramount. Game-theoretic TreeExplainer SHAP values prove that continuous assessment in weeks 6 through 11 drives over 92% of predictive outcome. In parallel, unsupervised K-Means segments students into three behavioral clusters: High Achievers, Diligent Improvers, and At-Risk students."*

---

### Scene 5: Executive PDF Report Generation (1:05 – 1:20)
- **Screen Action:** Navigate to **Institutional Reports**. Click **Export Multi-Page PDF Report**. Open the freshly generated 4-page PDF and briefly scroll through the benchmark audit specifications, confusion matrix, and governance disclosure.
- **Spoken Narration:**
  > *"With a single click, administrators generate an audit-ready, 4-page executive PDF report via jsPDF. It features verified benchmark provenance, confusion matrix diagnostics, holdout class imbalance disclosures, and IEEE 7000 algorithmic governance."*

---

### Scene 6: Architecture & Closing (1:20 – 1:30)
- **Screen Action:** Show the terminal running 100% passing tests (PyTest and Jest/API assertions). Return to the dashboard.
- **Spoken Narration:**
  > *"With full role-based access control, FERPA privacy isolation, and 100% test coverage, Trekverse AI exemplifies modern, production-grade AI engineering. Predict. Understand. Improve. Succeed."*
