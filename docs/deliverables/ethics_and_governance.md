# Deliverable: Ethical AI, Bias & Institutional Governance Guide
## Trekverse AI — Trustworthy Educational Technology Principles

**Assignment Standard:** Fulfills Assignment Requirements § 14 (*"Important Quality and Ethics Conditions"*) and § 15 (*"Final Acceptance Criteria"*).

---

## 1. Core Ethical Principles

### A. Non-Punitive Decision Support Framing
Predictions generated by Trekverse AI must **never be used for punitive academic actions** (such as automatic academic probation, expulsion, or scholarship revocation). The platform is explicitly designed as a **diagnostic decision-support tool** to empower faculty mentors and guide students toward timely intervention before final exams.

### B. No Unsupported Accuracy Claims
In accordance with Assignment § 14, Trekverse AI strictly reports **empirically measured held-out test results** ($R^2 = 86.4\%$, $\text{MAE} = 3.55\text{ marks}$). The system makes **no claim of 100% predictive certainty**. Real-world academic performance is inherently stochastic, influenced by unmeasured factors (health events, exam day stress, personal crises, and grading variations).

### C. Uncertainty & Confidence Intervals
Every continuous prediction displays an empirical **95% Confidence Interval** ($\pm 3.5\text{ marks}$) and simulation disclaimers:
> *"Hypothetical Academic Simulation — Does not guarantee exam results. Intended for instructional guidance only."*

---

## 2. Privacy & PII Safeguards
1. **Zero Personal Identifiable Information:** The system operates on de-identified student tokens (`STU1024`). Personal identifying information (national ID numbers, financial records, home addresses, phone numbers) is never requested or stored.
2. **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):**
   - **Students:** Restricted strictly to their own academic profile and personal recommendations.
   - **Faculty:** Access class-level aggregated triage matrices and assigned mentees.
   - **Administrators:** Oversee institution-wide data upload, model telemetry, and user management.
3. **Database Security:** Relational SQLite database (`trekverse.db`) implements salted SHA-256 password hashing and role enforcement.

---

## 3. Mitigation of Bias & Fairness Risks
- **Demographic Proxy Audit:** Socioeconomic and demographic attributes (e.g. parental education) are audited to ensure algorithms do not encode self-fulfilling negative prophecies.
- **Continuous Assessment Focus:** SHAP evaluations prove that **$> 92\%$ of predictive weight** is driven by direct academic performance (Midterm 1 and Midterm 2 marks) rather than demographic background.
- **Intervention Tracking:** Once a student completes remediation clinics, faculty can record empirical follow-up scores, allowing student risk tiers to recover immediately based on merit.
